thead, tbody and tfoot

HTML tables can be divided into three sections for better structure: <thead> (table header), <tbody> (table body), and <tfoot> (table footer).

Output:

Roll No Name Marks
1 Amit 80
2 Riya 85
3 Rahul 90
Total Students 3 

<table>

  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Roll No</th>
      <th>Name</th>
      <th>Marks</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>

  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>1</td>
      <td>Amit</td>
      <td>80</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>2</td>
      <td>Riya</td>
      <td>85</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>3</td>
      <td>Rahul</td>
      <td>90</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>

  <tfoot>
    <tr>
      <td colspan="2">Total Students</td>
      <td>3</td>
    </tr>
  </tfoot>

</table>
